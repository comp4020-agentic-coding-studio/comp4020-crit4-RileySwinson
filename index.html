Playing it

Click or drag
Plays the orbit under the pointer. Dragging bends the note.
Letter keys
Z–M and Q–P play the current point at different pitches, two rows like a keyboard.
Arrow keys
Nudge the current point. Hold Shift for finer steps.
Scroll, or Shift-drag
Zoom, and pan.
